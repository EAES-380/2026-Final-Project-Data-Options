# LTER Meteorological Stations at the Kellogg Biological Station, Michigan (1941–present)

**Metadata page:** https://portal.edirepository.org/nis/mapbrowse?packageid=knb-lter-kbs.2  
**DOI (example recent version):** https://doi.org/10.6073/pasta/f4744fe92e13bc84847fd8ff13435695

## Overview
Long-term meteorological observations from the Kellogg Biological Station LTER site in southwest Michigan. Includes both raw hourly data and quality-controlled daily and annual summaries.

## Key contents
- Daily and hourly air temperature, precipitation, wind speed and direction, solar radiation, relative humidity
- Soil temperature and moisture
- Synthetic complete records supplemented from nearby stations when needed

## Temporal & spatial coverage
- **Time:** 1941–present (most complete from 1988 onward)
- **Location:** Kellogg Biological Station, Hickory Corners, Michigan, USA

## Why this dataset is useful for final projects
Classic long-term climate record suitable for trend analysis, extreme-event characterization, or linking climate variables to ecosystem processes. Multiple temporal resolutions are provided.

## Suggested analyses
- Long-term temperature and precipitation trends
- Changes in extreme precipitation or heat events
- Relationships between climate variables and agricultural or ecological metrics (if paired with other KBS data)
