# Prospect Hill Hydrological Stations at Harvard Forest (since 2005)

**DOI:** (see metadata page for current version)

## Overview
Long-term hydrologic measurements on two small watersheds in the Prospect Hill Tract of the Harvard Forest, Massachusetts. Includes continuous automated stream and wetland water-level and discharge data.

## Key contents
- Streamflow and water-level data from weirs and pipes
- Contrasting wetland types (spruce-hemlock wetland vs. beaver swamp)
- Near-real-time updates available

## Temporal & spatial coverage
- **Time:** 2005–ongoing
- **Location:** Harvard Forest, Petersham, Massachusetts, USA

## Why this dataset is useful for final projects
Students can examine the role of headwater wetlands in regulating streamflow, compare adjacent watersheds with different topography and history, or study high-frequency hydrologic responses.

## Suggested analyses
- Wetland influence on streamflow dynamics
- Comparison of the two contrasting watersheds
- Event-based or seasonal hydrologic analysis
