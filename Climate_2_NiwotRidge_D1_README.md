# Climate Data from Niwot Ridge LTER D1 Station (High-Elevation Alpine)

**Metadata page:** https://portal.edirepository.org/nis/mapbrowse?packageid=knb-lter-nwt.273  
**DOI (example):** https://doi.org/10.6073/pasta/e06669d42e1314dd93160d091022a717

## Overview
High-frequency (10-minute) climatological data from the D1 climate station on Niwot Ridge, Colorado (elevation ~3738 m). Part of the Niwot Ridge LTER long-term monitoring program.

## Key contents
- Air temperature, relative humidity, wind speed and direction, solar radiation
- Quality-flagged native-resolution data
- Suitable for alpine climate and meteorology studies

## Temporal & spatial coverage
- **Time:** 2014–ongoing (10-minute resolution)
- **Location:** Niwot Ridge, Colorado Front Range, USA (high-elevation alpine)

## Why this dataset is useful for final projects
Students interested in alpine or mountain meteorology can examine high-elevation climate variability, wind patterns, or radiation regimes. High temporal resolution supports diurnal cycle analyses.

## Suggested analyses
- Diurnal and seasonal temperature/wind cycles
- Extreme wind or temperature events at high elevation
- Comparison with lower-elevation stations
